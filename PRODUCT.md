# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Jigsaw on PHP 8.4, a static site built from `main`. The current Jekyll site stays on `gh-pages` (tag `circa-2026` marks what is live) until the new one replaces it.

## Users

Not yet confirmed in detail. Working assumption: people who knew the site in its run and come back to rediscover it, the people it features and credits, and newcomers who land on a single piece from search or a shared link and stay to wander.

## Product Purpose

axisofstevil.com is a **living archive**: the preserved body of Axis of Stevil's work since 2005, made to be explored and rediscovered. It is on sabbatical, not posting, but it keeps the ability to release new pieces on its Monday, Wednesday and Friday cadence, so the archive can grow again without a redesign.

Success: a visitor can find their way into the work from any page, keeps going from one piece to the next, and the whole of it reads as one body of work rather than a stack of old posts.

## Positioning

Original Axis of Stevil content "was invented for this domain and has been in the market since 2005" (Authenticity page). It is one group's absurdist output over many years, in every form it took: writing, comics, Flash movies, music, photography, letters and a fake history of the organisation.

## Operating Context

- The work spans sections that have their own names on the site today: Words and pictures (publications), Vocabulary, People (Featured Steves), AMA (Ask Stevil), plus the History timeline and the site's own pages (Mission, History, Shirts, Store, Authenticity).
- Pieces were released as weekly issues, each with its own front page, on a Monday, Wednesday and Friday cadence. How the issues and front pages appear on the new site is undecided.
- Media (images, Flash movies, audio, video) is served from `archive.axisofstevil.com`, by full address.
- Flash movies are part of the work and are preserved; how they play on the new site is undecided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every published address on the current site keeps working or redirects.
- A missing page answers a real 404.
- Undecided: search; the join, unjoin and contact forms; comments; analytics; the feed; social links; the contact details.

## Brand Commitments

- **The name** Axis of Stevil and **the logo banner** (`img/logo-banner.png`, with its favicon) carry over as the mark.
- **"Random insanity at its finest."** The tagline, and the deadpan, mock-official voice of the copy: mission statements, authenticity guarantees and "true historical accounting" played straight.
- **"Look at Something Random"** stays a core way into the work: random discovery is part of the identity, not a widget.

## Evidence on Hand

- The full body of work, with its credits, dates, series and sections.
- The site's own pages: Mission, Authenticity, History (with its timeline), Shirts, Store, Contact, Join.
- The archive host `archive.axisofstevil.com` for every media file.
- No testimonials, press or audience figures are on hand; none should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. **The work leads.** The site is a way into the pieces; its own interface stays out of their way.
2. **Every piece is a door.** Any page, reached from anywhere, leads further into the archive.
3. **Played straight.** The absurdity lives in the content and the voice; the presentation is earnest, official and well made, which is the joke.
4. **Ready to resume.** Nothing in the design assumes the archive is finished.
